**Every WASM cell reproduces M8.1 byte for byte:** all seven realistic outputs and all boxes are
identical to M8.1's Chromium page-context run, in Chromium and in Linux Firefox. WebGPU outputs
differ in bytes, as expected for a different backend, and stay within the bound. The worst realistic
relErr in any cell is 4.51e-06 (Chrome WebGPU). The synthetic diagnostic stays at 1.04e-01 on
WASM, as in M8.1, and decides nothing.

**RE-1 re-scored on each cell's own boxes** (protocol §4.5): 0 / 306 sensitive glyphs exposed and
worst over-mask 0.38 in every completed mode, WebGPU included. The one mode with a missing launch
cannot establish cross-launch determinism, so its re-score is recorded as not passing. That is an
artifact of the missing launch; its completed launches expose nothing.

**Spread, not only the median:** the Chrome WASM headless cell's 30 warm inferences ran 467–846 ms
(p90 619 ms). Its per-launch medians were 513, 532 and 569 ms, so none meets the benchmark's
slow-mode rule (> 2× the fastest). The controlled benchmark below is the timing of record.

**First-inference cost differs by backend:** cold inference ~565 ms (WASM), 1.2–1.6 s (Chrome
WebGPU, shader compilation) and **12.3 s** (Firefox WebGPU). The last is measured, and nothing is
attributed.

**Against documented requirements:** QG-03 sets no memory or latency threshold, and none is
created. The constitution's 105 MB (T2) and 12.3 MB (T1) are projected **weights**, and TR-01's
weights are 4.8 MB. Its runtime arena, 131.1 MB, sits beside M7.1's measured 125.4 MB for
PP-OCRv5_mobile_det on this machine, the only prior text-detector figure. It is context, not a
bound.

## Output semantics and privacy

One float32 output `[1,1,H,W]` in every cell and backend. No STRING tensor; boxes carry only
`x, y, w, h, score` (G6 structural check, every cell). No recognition model is in the extension
or loaded. No foreign network request in any launch. Results hold hashes, statistics, timings and
geometry only.
