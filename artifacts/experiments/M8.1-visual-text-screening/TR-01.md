### Relationship to the rejected `PP-OCRv5_mobile_det`, stated as fact

TR-01's `inference.json` is **byte-identical** to the one pinned for `PP-OCRv5_mobile_det` in M7.1
(`05feef1a…`). Its converted ONNX has the same byte size (4 766 440 B) and the same node count (925),
with a different hash, because the weights differ. **TR-01 is the same network definition as the
rejected model, with different weights.** M8 inferred this from the parameter-file size; M8.1
measured it.

That does not reopen PP-OCRv5, which was not run, scored or used as a control here. Its verdict of
record stands under the criterion it was judged by. What it does mean: TR-01's evidence is about
**these weights under RE-1**, not about a different architecture.

## Output semantics and privacy (Part F)

The model emits one float32 map of per-pixel text probability. Boxes are derived by geometry (DB
post-processing, `thresh 0.3`, `box_thresh 0.6`, `unclip_ratio 1.5`, from its `inference.yml`).
No recognition model was downloaded or loaded, and none exists on this path. Evidence files hold
geometry and statistics only: a scan for every sensitive held-out string and the development
fixture's strings found none.
